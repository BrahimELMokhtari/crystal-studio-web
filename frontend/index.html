Export your figure

The ball legend stays at the upper left, 1 cm from the nearest crystal atom in the saved figure. The crystal is fitted to the figure while keeping your viewing angle. Choose Transparent for a figure without a background. PNG stores the DPI; PDF preserves the physical size and transparency. Up to 5,000 DPI, 8,192 pixels per side and 32 million pixels, within your device's limits. At 5,000 DPI, smaller physical figures are needed; use Fit size to device when necessary.

Your current rotation, zoom and pan are preserved, with no background or element legend. Cell, axes, connections and selected atoms follow your scene settings. Rectangular images keep the view proportions with transparent margins.

Place atoms at reference sites

Choose sites and assign an element to each. Opposite boundaries share one periodic site. These are reference positions, not space-group or Wyckoff-site generation; the chosen atoms can change the actual symmetry.

One atom per line: element x y z [occupancy]. All 118 element symbols are supported. Coordinates use 0-1; 1 wraps to 0. Occupancy is optional and defaults to 1. Enter the full unit-cell contents; symmetry-related atoms are not generated automatically.
